Understanding environmental challenges is the first step towards creating solutions.

Air Pollution

Vehicle emissions, industrial activities and burning fuels can affect air quality and human health.

SMART WASTE MANAGEMENT

Waste Less, Live Better

Proper waste segregation makes recycling easier and helps reduce environmental damage.

🍌

Wet / Organic Waste

Food scraps, fruit peels, vegetable waste and other suitable biodegradable materials.

♻️ Compost where possible
📦

Dry Recyclable Waste

Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans and accepted plastic packaging.

♻️ Keep recyclables clean and dry
🔋

Electronic Waste

Old phones, batteries, chargers and discarded electronic equipment.

♻️ Use authorised collection centres
Remember: Waste segregation rules and bin colours can differ by location. Follow your local waste collection guidelines.
